# Roasting Schedule

Maya Chen roasts Tuesday through Thursday, 6:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. The 12 kg Probat is warmed for 20 minutes before the first batch.

Weekly plan:
- Tuesday: wholesale base coffees. 3 batches of Horizon Blend, 2 of Decaf Moon.
- Wednesday: retail single origins. Rotate according to [[Cupping Notes]] and [[Bean Sourcing]].
- Thursday: wholesale top-ups and online orders. Roast-to-order cut-off is 10:00 a.m.

Rules:
- Minimum batch is 6 kg; maximum is 11 kg for a 12 kg drum.
- Rest coffee 3–5 days before retail sale and 5–7 days before espresso service.
- Record charge weight, drop temperature, development time, and roast date for every batch.
- Bag and label within 24 hours; see [[Packaging and Labels]].
- Clean the chaff collector after every roast day and log it in [[Equipment Maintenance]].

Wholesale orders over 50 lb need 48 hours' notice. Fulfillment steps are in [[Wholesale Order Fulfillment]].
