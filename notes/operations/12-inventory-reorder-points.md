# Inventory Reorder Points

Maya and Jonah review inventory every Monday at 8:30 a.m. Counts are kept in the shared inventory sheet. Reorder when stock hits the point below.

Green coffee:
- Horizon Blend components: 60 lb
- Decaf Moon: 30 lb
- Rotating single origin: 40 lb

Retail and café:
- 12 oz bags: 500 units
- 2 lb bags: 100 units
- 12 oz cups and lids: 2,000 each
- 16 oz cups and lids: 1,500 each
- Oat milk: 24 cartons
- Whole milk: 18 gallons
- Vanilla and caramel syrup: 12 bottles each
- Pastries: daily par by 2:00 p.m.

Cleaning and supplies: reorder at two weeks of cover. Do not stock more than six weeks of green coffee; cash is tight and freshness matters. Lead times are 7–10 days for packaging and 3–4 weeks for imported green coffee. See [[Cash Flow and Runway]] and [[Bean Sourcing]].
