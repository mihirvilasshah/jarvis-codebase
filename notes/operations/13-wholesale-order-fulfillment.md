# Wholesale Order Fulfillment

Luis Ortega owns wholesale accounts. Orders arrive by email or the wholesale order form before 10:00 a.m. Tuesday for Thursday delivery.

Fulfillment steps:
1. Confirm the order and delivery window within two hours.
2. Check roast availability against [[Roasting Schedule]].
3. Pull, weigh, and label cases. Include a roast-date sticker and brew guide.
4. Pack 5 lb bags in cases of five; use the compostable liners in [[Packaging and Labels]].
5. Print the invoice and packing slip. Payment terms are net 15.
6. Load the van by route. Deliveries run Thursday 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.
7. Send delivery confirmation and a one-line brew tip.

Account standards:
- Minimum opening order is 20 lb.
- Free delivery within 5 miles; otherwise $12 per drop.
- Out-of-stock items are back-ordered unless the customer says otherwise.
- Report quality issues to Maya within 24 hours.

Pricing is in [[Pricing and Margins]]; prospecting is in [[Wholesale Outreach]].
