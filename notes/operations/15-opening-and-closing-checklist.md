# Opening and Closing Checklist

Opening (manager on duty, 30 minutes before service):
- Disarm alarm, turn on lights, and check for leaks or damage.
- Start the espresso machine and verify pressure at 9 bar.
- Calibrate the grinder and pull a test shot.
- Check pastry case, milk dates, and sanitizer buckets.
- Count the till to $200 and confirm the card terminal is online.
- Unlock the front door at 7:00 a.m. weekdays, 8:00 a.m. weekends. See [[Location and Hours]].

Closing:
- Stop espresso service 15 minutes before close and offer last call.
- Backflush the machine and clean the grinder; see [[Equipment Maintenance]].
- Wrap pastries, label date, and refrigerate or discard.
- Count the till, close Square, and drop the deposit.
- Take out compost and trash; wipe tables and sanitize the bar.
- Set the alarm and lock both doors.

Report issues in the shift log. Safety incidents go to Jonah immediately and are documented under [[Workplace Safety]].
