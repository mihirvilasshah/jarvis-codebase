# Equipment Maintenance

Jonah Reed owns the maintenance calendar. Log every service in the equipment binder and the shared sheet.

Daily:
- Backflush the espresso machine at close.
- Clean grinder hoppers and purge burrs.
- Wipe the Probat cooling tray and check the chaff collector.
- Check refrigerator and freezer temperatures; record at open and close.

Weekly:
- Deep-clean the espresso machine group heads.
- Vacuum the roaster's drop chute and inspect the flame sensor.
- Descale the hot water tower.

Monthly:
- Replace espresso machine gaskets and shower screens.
- Inspect cords, plugs, and the fire extinguisher gauge.
- Clean condenser coils on the walk-in.

Quarterly: Probat technician inspection, including thermocouple calibration and safety valves. Budget $2,250 per visit. Annual: hood cleaning and backflow test.

If equipment fails, switch to the backup grinder and call the vendor. Do not operate the roaster if the flame sensor or exhaust is faulty. See [[Workplace Safety]] and [[Opening and Closing Checklist]].
