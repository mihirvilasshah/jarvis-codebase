# Cupping Notes

Cupping happens every Wednesday at 10:00 a.m. in the roastery. Maya leads; Sam and any interested staff join. No perfume or strong coffee before the table.

Protocol:
- 8.25 g coffee per 150 ml water at 93°C.
- 4-minute steep, break the crust at 4:00, skim at 6:00.
- Slurp at 8–10 minutes and again at 15 minutes as it cools.
- Score aroma, acidity, sweetness, body, flavor, aftertaste, and balance.

Scoring: use the SCA form, 6–10 scale per attribute. Anything below 80 is not served. 84+ can be a single origin; 86+ can carry a premium price.
