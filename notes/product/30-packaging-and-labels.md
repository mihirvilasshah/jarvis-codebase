# Packaging and Labels

All retail coffee is packed in compostable kraft bags with a one-way valve. Labels are printed in-house on the thermal printer.

Required label fields:
- Northwind Roasters and logo
- Coffee name and roast level
- Origin: farm or cooperative, region, altitude, process
- Tasting notes
- Net weight (12 oz / 340 g or 2 lb / 907 g)
- Roast date and best-by date (best within 8 weeks)
- Brew guidance and website
- Batch number for traceability

Standards:
- Print labels the same day as roasting; see [[Roasting Schedule]].
- Apply labels straight and centered, 2 cm from the top seam.
- Check weight on the scale: 340 g ± 5 g.
- Wholesale cases use plain cases with a roast-date sticker and brew card. See [[Wholesale Order Fulfillment]].
- Keep a label sample book for each origin; update when [[Bean Sourcing]] changes.

Sustainability: bags are compostable, labels are paper, and shipping boxes are recycled. Do not put stickers over the valve. Marketing copy follows [[Brand Voice Guide]].
