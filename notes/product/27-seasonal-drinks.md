# Seasonal Drinks

Seasonal drinks launch four times a year and run for eight weeks. Elena and Sam develop them; Maya approves the coffee pairing.

Winter (December–January):
- Maple Cardamom Latte — $6.25
- Orange Mocha — $6.50
- Honey Lavender Cold Brew — $6.25

Spring (March–April):
- Strawberry Matcha — $6.50
- Lemon Bergamot Americano — $5.75
- Vanilla Rose Cold Brew — $6.25

Summer (June–July):
- Coconut Cold Brew — $6.00
- Peach Iced Tea Latte — $6.25
- Espresso Tonic — $6.50

Autumn (September–October):
- Pumpkin Chai Latte — $6.25
- Apple Butter Cortado — $5.50
- Maple Pecan Cold Brew — $6.25

Development rules:
- Test with 20 customers; keep a drink only if 70% would order again.
- Cost ingredients at 30% or less of price. See [[Pricing and Margins]].
- Two-week trial in the POS; track attach rate and waste.
- Add allergens and caffeine notes to the menu. See [[Coffee Menu]].
