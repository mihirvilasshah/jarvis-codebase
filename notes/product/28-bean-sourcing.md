# Bean Sourcing

Maya Chen owns green coffee buying. We buy through three importers: Sweet Harvest, Cascade Green, and Origin Direct.

Current lineup:
- **Horizon Blend** — 60% washed Colombia Huila, 40% natural Brazil Cerrado. Notes: cocoa, caramel, orange. Our volume workhorse.
- **Decaf Moon** — sugarcane-process Colombia decaf. Notes: plum, almond, brown sugar.
- **Rotating single origin** — currently washed Ethiopia Guji, 1,950 masl. Notes: peach, jasmine, bergamot.

Standards:
- No purchase without a sample cupping score of 84+; see [[Cupping Notes]].
- Contract 60–80% of expected annual volume to lock price.
- Keep at least two origins in reserve for menu continuity.
- Visit at least one producer or importer partner per year when budget allows.
- Publish farm, region, altitude, and process on every bag. See [[Packaging and Labels]].

Pricing pressure: green coffee costs rose 11% in 2025. We will absorb half and pass half through in January. See [[Pricing and Margins]] and [[2026 Budget]]. Reorder levels are in [[Inventory Reorder Points]].
