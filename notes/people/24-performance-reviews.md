# Performance Reviews

We run light, regular reviews instead of one annual surprise. Jonah and Maya own the process.

Cadence:
- 30-day check-in for new hires.
- 90-day review with a written summary.
- Twice-yearly reviews in April and October.
- Monthly one-on-ones, 25 minutes, focused on blockers and growth.

Review inputs:
- Self-reflection form (three wins, one challenge, one goal).
- Attendance and reliability.
- Craft and quality: drink standards, roast consistency, or account health.
- Customer and teammate feedback.
- Safety and hygiene compliance.

Ratings: exceeds, meets, developing. Ratings are not tied to a forced curve.

Compensation: merit increases are considered in April and are budgeted in [[2026 Budget]]. Promotions use a written scope and a 90-day trial. Barista progression is described in [[Barista Training]].

Conversations are documented and signed by employee and manager. Records are confidential and stored in the locked cabinet. See [[Hiring Process]].
