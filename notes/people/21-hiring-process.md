# Hiring Process

Jonah Reed leads hiring with Maya for roasting roles. We hire for warmth, reliability, and curiosity; coffee experience is a plus, not a requirement.

Steps:
1. Role scorecard with must-haves and nice-to-haves.
2. Post on our website, Indeed, and local job boards for 10 days.
3. Screen applications for availability and legal work status.
4. 20-minute phone screen with Jonah.
5. Paid working interview: 2 hours on the bar or in production, $22 per hour.
6. Reference checks: two professional references.
7. Offer letter with rate, schedule, and benefits. Background check only where required.

Onboarding:
- First day: paperwork, tour, safety walkthrough, and menu tasting.
- First two weeks: [[Barista Training]] or roasting assistant training.
- 30-day check-in and 90-day review; see [[Performance Reviews]].

We do not ask about salary history. Accommodations are available on request. Hiring paperwork is kept in the locked file cabinet; see [[Licenses and Insurance]].
