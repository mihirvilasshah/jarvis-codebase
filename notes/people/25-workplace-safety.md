# Workplace Safety

Jonah Reed is the safety lead. Everyone is responsible for speaking up.

Hazards:
- Hot surfaces: roaster drum, cooling tray, steam wands, and kettles.
- Moving parts: grinder burrs, roaster drum, and cooling tray arms.
- Chemicals: sanitizer, descaler, and oven cleaner.
- Slips and burns in the bar and dish area.
- Delivery lifting and van driving.

Rules:
- Closed-toe shoes, no loose sleeves near the roaster.
- Use heat gloves for the cooling tray and never reach into the drum.
- Never clean a grinder while it is plugged in.
- Keep aisles clear and mop spills immediately.
- Two-person lift for anything over 40 lb.
- No employee works alone in the roastery after 6:00 p.m.

Incidents: stop work, provide first aid, notify Jonah, and complete an incident report within 24 hours. Call 911 for emergencies. First aid kit is by the back door; extinguisher is by the roaster.
