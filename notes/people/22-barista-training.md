# Barista Training

Sam Okafor owns training. New baristas complete 24 hours of paid training over two weeks before working solo.

Week 1:
- Espresso fundamentals: dose, distribution, tamp, and shot timing.
- Milk steaming and pouring: target 55–60°C.
- Menu build and modifiers; see [[Coffee Menu]].
- POS, cash handling, and [[Loyalty Program]].
- Food safety, allergen awareness, and [[Workplace Safety]].

Week 2:
- Speed and sequencing during a mock rush.
- Opening and closing routines; see [[Opening and Closing Checklist]].
- Grinder calibration and dial-in with the day's coffee.
- Customer recovery and accessibility basics.
- Final practical: 12 drinks to standard in 15 minutes.

Certification:
- Trainer signs off on espresso, milk, register, and safety.
- New hires shadow two weekend shifts before solo closing.
- Retraining every six months and after any menu change.

Training records are kept by Jonah. Scheduling during training follows [[Shift Scheduling]].
