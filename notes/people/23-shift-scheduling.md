# Shift Scheduling

Jonah Reed publishes the schedule every Thursday for the following two weeks using Homebase.

Coverage rules:
- Minimum two people on the floor at all times; three from 8:00 to 11:00 a.m. on weekdays.
- One lead barista or manager on every shift.
- Roasting days need one roasting assistant; see [[Roasting Schedule]].
- Farmers markets need two staff; see [[Farmers Market Plan]].

Availability:
- Submit changes at least 14 days ahead.
- Shift swaps must be approved by a manager and keep overtime at zero.
- No clopens: at least 10 hours between shifts.

Time off: request 21 days ahead for vacations, 48 hours for appointments. Two weeks of paid vacation for full-time staff after one year.

Fairness: weekend shifts rotate monthly. Overtime must be pre-approved. During illness, staff must stay home; coverage comes from the on-call list.

Labor budget is 30% of revenue in [[2026 Budget]]. Payroll deadlines are in [[Payroll Schedule]].
