# Instagram Content Calendar

Elena Rossi runs @northwindroasters with support from Sam. Goal: 4 posts and 3 stories per week, focused on education, people, and new coffee.

Weekly rhythm:
- Monday: menu or new coffee photo. Pair with [[Coffee Menu]].
- Wednesday: behind-the-scenes reel — roasting, cupping, or packing. See [[Roasting Schedule]].
- Friday: staff or customer story. Tag with permission.
- Saturday: brewing tip or event reminder. Link to [[Farmers Market Plan]] when relevant.
- Stories: daily coffee special, poll, and repost of tagged photos.

Monthly:
- First week: origin spotlight from [[Bean Sourcing]].
- Third week: loyalty program reminder; see [[Loyalty Program]].
- Last week: review insights, save top-performing formats, and schedule the next month.

Rules:
- Post between 8:00 and 10:00 a.m. Pacific.
- Alt text on every image.
- No reposts without credit.
- Respond to comments within one business day.

Voice and caption structure are in [[Brand Voice Guide]]. Photography: natural light, no heavy filters.
