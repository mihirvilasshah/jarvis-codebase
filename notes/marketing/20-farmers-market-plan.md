# Farmers Market Plan

We attend the Alberta Street Market on the second and fourth Saturdays, May through October, 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. Booth fee is $55 per market.

Setup:
- 10x10 tent, two tables, banner, and menu board.
- Batch brew in 3 dispensers: drip, decaf, and a seasonal option from [[Seasonal Drinks]].
- Retail bags: 40 units of two origins, plus a 2 lb option. See [[Coffee Menu]].
- Card reader with offline mode, cash drawer with $150, and QR code for [[Loyalty Program]].
- Sampling cups and a water station.

Staffing: two people per market, scheduled in [[Shift Scheduling]]. One must be a lead barista.

Goals: $900 average sales, 60 email sign-ups, and 15 new loyalty members per market. Track sales, sign-ups, and best sellers in the market log.

Weather: cancel if lightning or sustained rain; post on Instagram by 7:00 a.m. Use [[Instagram Content Calendar]] for promotion. Pack out all waste and leave the spot clean.
