# Brand Voice Guide

Northwind sounds warm, curious, and plainspoken. We are coffee experts, not coffee snobs.

Voice principles:
- **Specific over flowery.** Say "washed Ethiopian, notes of peach and jasmine" instead of "a journey of flavor."
- **Short sentences.** One idea per sentence.
- **Invite, don't lecture.** Explain brewing simply and link to a guide.
- **Local and human.** Name neighbours, makers, and staff when appropriate.

Words we use: bright, sweet, balanced, cozy, everyday, small-batch, transparent.
Words we avoid: artisanal, curated, elevated, disrupt, guilt-free.

Formatting:
- Instagram captions start with a concrete image, then one useful detail, then a question.
- Emails sign off with "See you at the bar — the Northwind team."
- Product descriptions are 40–70 words and always include origin and roast level.

Tone shifts slightly for wholesale: more practical, less playful. See [[Wholesale Outreach]] and [[Instagram Content Calendar]].
