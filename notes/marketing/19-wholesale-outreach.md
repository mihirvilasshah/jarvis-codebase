# Wholesale Outreach

Luis Ortega owns wholesale growth. Target: add 12 accounts in 2026, with an average of 35 lb per month.

Ideal accounts: independent cafés, co-working spaces, boutique hotels, and offices within 8 miles of the roastery. Avoid accounts that need same-day delivery or exclusive menu control.

Outreach sequence:
1. Visit in person with a 12 oz sample and a one-page wholesale sheet.
2. Follow up by email within 48 hours with pricing and a brew guide.
3. Offer a paid tasting at their location or ours.
4. Send a two-week trial at 10% off; no free cases.
5. On close, schedule first delivery and training.

Pipeline is tracked in the shared CRM sheet: lead, sampled, trial, active, paused. Review every Friday.

Materials: current [[Pricing and Margins]], [[Wholesale Order Fulfillment]] standards, and [[Bean Sourcing]] stories. Keep claims accurate and never promise exclusivity without Maya's approval. Voice guidance is in [[Brand Voice Guide]].
