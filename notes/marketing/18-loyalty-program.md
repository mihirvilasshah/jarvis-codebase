# Loyalty Program

The Northwind Regulars program launched in 2024 and has 1,840 active members. It runs on Square Loyalty.

How it works:
- Earn 1 point per $1 spent in the café.
- 100 points = one free 12 oz drink.
- Double points on Mondays and on reusable-cup purchases.
- Birthday reward: any drink, valid for 14 days.
- Bean subscribers earn 2 points per $1 and free local delivery.

Costs and controls:
- Reward redemption is about 4.2% of café sales, within the 5% budget in [[2026 Budget]].
- Staff must scan the customer's number before applying a reward. No manual points.
- Fraud checks monthly: duplicate accounts, unusual point bursts, and expired rewards.

Growth plan:
- Add a "bring a friend" referral in Q1.
- Test a wholesale perk: free brew consultation after three orders. Coordinate with [[Wholesale Outreach]].
- Promote at the farmers market; see [[Farmers Market Plan]].
