# Payroll Schedule

Payroll is processed by Priya Nair through Gusto. Employees are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and the last business day of the month.

Key dates:
- Timesheets are due to Jonah by 5:00 p.m. two business days before payday.
- Priya approves payroll one business day before payday.
- Direct deposits post by 9:00 a.m. on payday.

Rates and rules:
- Portland minimum wage applies to all hourly staff; tipped baristas earn an additional tip pool share.
- Overtime must be approved in writing by Jonah before it is worked. See [[Shift Scheduling]].
- Tips are pooled weekly and distributed by hours worked.
- Mileage for wholesale deliveries is reimbursed at the IRS rate; submit receipts within 30 days.

Contractor invoices (bookkeeper, roaster technician) are paid with the second payroll of the month. Payroll taxes and workers' comp are included in [[2026 Budget]]. Questions about pay go to Priya, not to coworkers.
