# Bookkeeping Checklist

Priya Nair owns bookkeeping with support from our contract bookkeeper, Dana Whitfield. Books close on the 5th of the following month.

Weekly:
- Reconcile Square deposits to the bank account.
- Enter wholesale invoices and mark payments received.
- File receipts in the shared drive by vendor and month.
- Review uncategorized expenses.

Monthly:
- Reconcile the credit card and line of credit.
- Record payroll journal entries from Gusto; tie to [[Payroll Schedule]].
- Review inventory counts with Maya; see [[Inventory Reorder Points]].
- Compare actuals to [[2026 Budget]] and flag variances over $500.
- Send wholesale statements and follow up on invoices over 30 days.

Quarterly:
- File and pay Oregon CAT and Portland business taxes.
- Review sales tax collected on retail beans and merchandise.
- Update the fixed asset list, including the Probat and delivery van.

Year-end: provide 1099s to contractors by January 31. Keep records for seven years.
