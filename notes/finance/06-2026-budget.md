# 2026 Budget

Prepared by Priya Nair. Approved by Maya Chen on December 12, 2025. The plan targets $1.18M in revenue and a 9% net margin.

Revenue plan:
- Café retail: $732,000 (62%)
- Wholesale: $448,000 (38%)
- Events and classes: included in café retail

Major expenses:
- Cost of goods sold: $401,000 (34% of revenue)
- Payroll and benefits: $354,000 (30%)
- Rent: $58,200
- Utilities and internet: $21,600
- Marketing: $24,000
- Equipment maintenance: $9,000
- Insurance and licenses: $14,400
- Software and card fees: $18,000

Capital plan: $35,000 for a used delivery van and $8,000 for café seating. We will fund the van from cash flow, not debt. See [[Cash Flow and Runway]] for the monthly view and [[Pricing and Margins]] for assumptions. Actuals are reviewed monthly against [[Bookkeeping Checklist]].
