# Cash Flow and Runway

We keep a minimum operating balance of $60,000 in the business checking account. As of November 1, 2026, the balance is $84,200 and there is a $25,000 undrawn line of credit.

Monthly fixed costs are about $38,500: rent $4,850, payroll $29,500, utilities $1,800, software $1,500, and insurance $850. Variable costs are roughly 34% of sales.

Cash rhythm:
- Wholesale invoices are net 15. About 70% of accounts pay on time.
- Café card settlements arrive in two business days.
- Green coffee is paid 50% deposit at contract and 50% before shipment.
- Payroll runs on the 15th and last business day; see [[Payroll Schedule]].

Runway rule: if cash falls below $60,000, pause non-essential spending and delay capital purchases. The 2026 van purchase is contingent on two consecutive months above $75,000. See [[2026 Budget]] and [[Inventory Reorder Points]] for the cash tied up in stock.
