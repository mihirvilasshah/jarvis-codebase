# Pricing and Margins

Our target gross margin is 68% on retail drinks and 45% on wholesale beans. Prices are reviewed every January and after any green coffee increase above 8%.

Retail pricing logic:
- 12 oz latte: $5.25. Cost is $1.55, margin 70%.
- 16 oz drip: $3.50. Cost is $0.72, margin 79%.
- 12 oz retail beans: $19.00. Cost is $9.10, margin 52%.
- Pastry: 55% margin; we bake in-house on weekends.

Wholesale pricing:
- 5 lb bag: $62.00.
- 25 lb case: $285.00.
- Accounts ordering 100+ lb per month receive 8% off.
- Delivery is free within 5 miles; otherwise $12 per drop.

Discounts: staff get 50% off drinks and 30% off beans. Loyalty rewards are tracked separately in [[Loyalty Program]]. Never discount below 35% gross margin without Maya's approval. Menu details are in [[Coffee Menu]].
