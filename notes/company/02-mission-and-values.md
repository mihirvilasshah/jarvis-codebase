# Mission and Values

Our mission is to make exceptional coffee feel approachable, while paying farmers and staff fairly.

Values:
- **Craft first.** We cup every roast before it reaches a customer. See [[Cupping Notes]].
- **Radical transparency.** We publish origin details and roast dates. [[Packaging and Labels]] explains the labels.
- **Neighbourhood hospitality.** We learn regulars' names and support local makers.
- **Sustainable by default.** We use compostable retail bags, recycle chaff, and discount drinks for customers with reusable cups.

Decision rules:
1. If a change lowers quality, we do not ship it.
2. Staff safety and fair scheduling come before sales targets. See [[Shift Scheduling]].
3. We test small before committing cash. New drinks go through a two-week trial; see [[Seasonal Drinks]].

Our values show up in hiring ([[Hiring Process]]) and in how we talk to customers ([[Brand Voice Guide]]).
