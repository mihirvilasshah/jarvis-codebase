# Business Overview

Northwind Roasters is a specialty coffee roastery and café at 418 NE Alberta Street in Portland, Oregon. We opened in March 2019 and currently employ 12 people: 5 full-time and 7 part-time.

The business has two revenue lines. Retail café sales make up about 62% of revenue, and wholesale coffee sales to local restaurants, offices, and grocers make up the remaining 38%. Our 2026 revenue target is $1.18M.

We roast in small batches on a 12 kg Probat and sell whole bean coffee online and at the café. Our differentiator is transparent sourcing: every bag lists the farm, region, altitude, and processing method.
